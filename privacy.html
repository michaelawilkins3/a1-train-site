Privacy policy

A1-train · Last updated November 2, 2026

What A1-train is

A1-train is a scheduling tool used by a single personal trainer on their own computer. It is not offered to the public.

Google data it uses

With the trainer's permission, A1-train uses one Google scope, calendar.events, to create and update training-session events on the trainer's Google Calendar and invite the client by email. It does not read the trainer's other calendar events, email, contacts, or files.

What is stored, and where

Client names, email addresses, and session dates are stored only on the trainer's computer. The Google sign-in is kept in the computer's secure keychain. Nothing is stored on any server run by A1-train, because there isn't one.

A calendar invite contains only the session title, dates and times, and the client's email address. Calendar events are then handled by Google under Google's privacy policy.

Sharing

Data is never sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising. A1-train's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing data

Clients can ask the trainer to delete their information at any time. The trainer can revoke A1-train's Google access at any time from their Google Account under Security → Third-party apps.
