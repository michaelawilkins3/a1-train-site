A1-train

A private scheduling tool for one personal trainer.

A1-train runs on the trainer's own computer. It keeps track of clients, training packages and attendance, and sends each client a Google Calendar invite for their recurring training sessions.

It isn't a public service: there are no accounts to create and nothing to sign up for. The only Google permission it asks for is to create and update events on the trainer's own calendar.
